# Product Design Incremental

Noted 19 October 2026, after the comparable survey. Store pages below were read that day. They describe what each game advertises. They do not describe how it feels in the hands.

## The subject

Advertising and marketing agencies are out.

The studio sells digital design, specifically UI/UX and product design, and leadership services along with that work. The closest existing studio game is still [Design Studio Tycoon](https://studio-tycoon.yashbanka.com/). Its author calls it a rudimentary mechanics exercise, and the live page is a five-minute session: hire, ship, survive the market, finish with the biggest book of business. [Author note](https://www.yashbanka.com/ai)

That theme is the neighbor. The length is not. The game wanted here is a fun incremental you stay with, not a five-minute round.

A follow-up search for a game about being a UI, UX, or product designer did not find one. The hits were people designing the interface of idle games, which is the opposite subject. No released incremental was verified whose work is screens, flows, and design leadership.

Digging A Hole is not labeled an incremental on its store page. Black Hole and First Squeeze both call themselves short. Find The Needle and the Lemonade sequel are the ones that advertise a long climb. The request was for that kind of fun incremental, held up against a five-minute studio round. "Short" on these pages still means an evening, not five minutes.

## What that pattern is doing

This is a reading of the four pages, not a design for the studio game.

Each one gives you one verb and a joke that stays literal. You look for a needle, dig a hole, feed a hole, sell lemonade. The first minutes are you doing that verb. Money buys a tool that does more of the verb. Later the tool is a line, a factory, or a government, and the original verb is still the joke. Find The Needle says the quiet part: you start by hand and end when you no longer have to.

A product-design incremental in that shape would start with one designer doing one UI or product-design task by hand. Upgrades would be the practice growing: a second pair of hands, a critique, a design system, a lead, a leadership engagement. The joke stays the work. It does not become a billboard empire or a five-minute score attack.

Leadership, on this reading, is a later tier of the same verb. Lemonade's sequel puts a workforce and then a presidency on top of selling lemonade. The presidency is not design leadership. The structure is the useful part: the job gets bigger than the person, and the early choices are still in the machine.

Design Studio Tycoon already has the studio noun and the income-per-second counter. It spends both inside five minutes. These references spend the same joke across a tech tree, a deepening hole, a few hundred upgrades, or a campaign.

## Conclusion

Research stops here. A targeted pass of Steam, itch.io, Google Play, the App Store, and developer sites did not find a released incremental about a UI/UX and product-design studio that also sells leadership. Design Studio Tycoon is the nearest studio game, and it is a five-minute mechanics exercise. The long, funny incrementals named as the feel are about hay, a hole, a black hole, and lemonade.

That is enough to say the idea can be worked on. It is not a copy of a game this search found. It is not a claim that no such game exists anywhere, or that anyone will want to play it. Those were not the questions.

The next question is a design question, not another store search: how a brief, a revision, and a leadership retainer feel as upgrades inside a long incremental. The survey behind this note is in [[Service Business Incremental Games]].
