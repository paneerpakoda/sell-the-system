# Hand, then the system, then the process

Supersedes [0004](0004-studio-run.md). [0002](0002-one-gesture-not-a-design-tool.md) and [0003](0003-fundamentals-then-tools.md) still hold for the gesture: colour, corner radius, and contrast, set by hand, and no freeform editor. Figma, paper.design, Layers, and Leadership as a Service are not in this design.

The short form is three stages. First you do the UI by hand. Then a team, a design system, and industry languages take the lines you keep judging. Then UX requirements arrive, and a design process pre-solves them. The win is one full month of 45 seconds that began only after Stage 3 was already active, with a big client shipped, no Project opened or fixed by hand, and month-end payroll paid in full. The banner does not stop the game.

A design system and a design process are how the game teaches that repetitive judgment can leave your hands. A token covers a vague property. A language covers one industry. A process step covers one UX requirement. What is covered is no longer a judgment you make.

Rejected: one Industry for the whole Run, from 0004, because every Project brings an industry and a language is codified per industry. Rejected: a Machine that copies the last ship and a retune fee, because the tokens and the language are the recipe. Rejected: a month counted in ships, because payroll is wall-clock. Rejected: a win that ends the Run, because the banner lets play continue.

Consequences. An Intern or a Permanent misses only on a judged property nothing covers, and a fully covered brief is always clean. At most one big client may be waiting until a Project ships hands-free. Hands-free is a clean auto-ship, or a ship you send without editing because the tokens, a language, or the process already met every judged requirement. Opening a miss and fixing it is a hand ship, and it does not lift the cap. Rolls that would break the cap arrive as small clients. While Stage 3 is active, a hand ship pays $10, a clean auto-ship pays $6, and a big client pays $48.
