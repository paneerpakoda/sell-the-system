# Grade and the client axes

[0005](0005-hand-then-system-then-process.md) still holds. Colour, corner radius, and contrast stay set by hand, then a system, then a process. Figma, paper.design, Layers, and Leadership as a Service are not in this design.

Every Project is Exacting or Loose, and Sound or Sour. Exacting or Loose decides the fee. Sound or Sour decides whether an On time Fit lowers a miss. The Screen's Grade is the lowest of colour, radius, and contrast. A Fit is that Grade at Best practice, with Type and Flow met when those rows exist. The Industry colour is Best practice, another calm swatch is Bare minimum, Hot and Lime are Wrong, and colour has no Acceptable. The Industry pair is Best practice, component radius with the background 4 larger. Any other shared pair stepped by 4 is Acceptable, including pairs outside 12–24. Otherwise a component radius greater than the background, a radius outside 12–24, or a lone 0, is Wrong, and the rest of that broken set is Bare minimum. Editorial 0 inside 4 is Best practice. Contrast is measured on painted ink, not the slider. At least 7 is Best practice, at least 4.5 and under 7 is Acceptable, at least 3 and under 4.5 is Bare minimum, and under 3 is Wrong.

The Designer reads the grades in the Wiki, on the Industry of the open Project. A Requirement shows its Grade, and Match sits beside it. A Head start offers the Background colour from a Fit that was not Sour.

Rejected: a flat fee, because Exacting or Loose decides the fee. Rejected: one axis for the fee and the miss, because Sound or Sour decides whether an On time Fit lowers a miss. Rejected: treating On time as the Month, because payroll is the Month and the miss reward is On time.

Consequences. A Loose Project pays $6, or $10 in Stage 3, when every named visual Requirement is Bare minimum or better and Type and Flow are met. A big Loose Project in that state pays $36, or $48 in Stage 3. An Exacting Project pays that same full fee only when the Screen's Grade is Best practice and Type and Flow are met. An Exacting Project that is not held and is not Best practice pays $3, or $6 in Stage 3, even when big. Ship is held, and pays no fee, when a named Requirement is Wrong, when any visual Requirement is Wrong on an Exacting client, or when Type or Flow is unmet. On time is 45 seconds. The miss reward fires only for a Sound client, on a successful ship, while On time was still above 0. It sets the miss of the Intern who attempted that Project to 0.2, or banks 0.2 for the next hire if no Intern has ever been hired. A Sour Fit pays its fee and does not change a miss. The reward does not add money and does not change the work interval.
