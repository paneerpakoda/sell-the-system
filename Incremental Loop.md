# Incremental Loop

The current design is the [[Game Design Document]]. This note is the 19 October 2026 reading of Aarimous’s advice on making an incremental, the same person behind A Game About Feeding A Black Hole. It is not a transcript. Where this note and the design document differ, the design document wins.

## One verb

The loop has to be obvious in the first minute. A broken screen is the job, and the job is UI. You set the colour, the corner radius, and the contrast, then ship the screen. The client pays for that UI. UX, the design process, and the methods of that practice come later. A client list, a pitch, or a second currency would hide the opening.

## Hand, then hand it off

You do each adjustment yourself. That is the start, and it should stop being the only way the work gets done.

A shipped design pays a flat amount. There is no upgrade that makes one click worth more. A System takes one adjustment: corners, then colour, then contrast. When all three are running, the studio ships without you, and the rate on screen shows it. The thing being sold stays the design.

## Feel strong, then meet a harder screen

Getting the first screen tidy should feel like you have won. Then the next Layer arrives already broken in a way the current setup cannot clear in one press. You go hunt the next upgrade to feel that strong again. That push and pull is the length. A longer list of the same percentage raise is not.

Upgrades come in two weights. Most are quiet: a bit more money, a junior who is worse than you. A few are spikes that change the job: Figma, the Sale, the next Layer. Those spikes are the novelty. They are not extra multipliers wearing a new name.

## Theme

The useful warning is not to look like a default cookie clicker. This game already has its theme: a product-design studio, then the System, then leadership sold as a service. Dark fantasy is someone else’s way of standing out. It is not this game.

## When to stop

Once those beats have been used, stop adding upgrades and ship. A tight hour or two is the length. That is the evening these games mean by short. It is not five minutes, and it is not a tree of three hundred of the same raise.
